Location-Based SEO Strategy — Queensland, Australia

A real-world SEO experiment designed and executed for Mr. Treadmill, an Australian fitness equipment retailer based in Brisbane, Queensland. This repository documents the strategy, execution, and results of a city-specific landing page initiative to capture unaddressed local search demand across Queensland.

🎯 The Problem

Mr. Treadmill had one generic hire page targeting Brisbane. Despite strong brand presence in Queensland, the site was invisible for local searches from other major Queensland cities. People in Gold Coast, Sunshine Coast, Toowoomba, and surrounding areas searching for treadmill hire had no city-specific content to land on, so they went to competitors.

💡 The Hypothesis

If we create dedicated landing pages for each Queensland city with locally relevant content and strong local intent signals, Google will rank them for city-specific hire queries, capturing demand that was previously going unaddressed.

🛠️ The Strategy

Phase 1 — Research

Identified high-volume, low-competition city-specific keywords across Queensland
Analyzed competitor pages in each city to understand content gaps
Mapped search intent: people searching "treadmill hire Gold Coast" want a local service, not a generic national page

Phase 2 — Page Creation

Built dedicated landing pages for each target city
Each page included city-specific keywords in title, headings, meta description, and body content
Added local relevance signals: references to delivery zones, local landmarks, and service areas
Implemented schema markup for local business signals
Built internal links from the main hire page and related content

Phase 3 — Monitoring and Scaling

Tracked performance in Google Search Console by page and query
Validated the model with initial results before scaling to new cities
Identified and resolved a keyword cannibalization issue between two Gold Coast URLs (blog post vs. hire page) — consolidated via canonical tag

🔍 Key Learnings

What worked:

City-specific pages dramatically outperform generic pages for local intent queries
High CTR on Sunshine Coast (14.24%) confirms strong intent match between page content and search query
Toowoomba at 12.15% CTR shows the model scales beyond major cities

What to watch:

Gold Coast position (15.87) is still improving after resolving a cannibalization issue between two competing URLs
Brisbane hire page has high impressions (5,903) but low CTR (1.64%) and low position (19.12) — indicates strong demand but more optimization needed

Cannibalization fix:

Identified two Gold Coast URLs competing for the same keywords: /treadmill-hire-gold-coast/ (hire page) and /gold-coast-treadmill-hire/ (blog post)
Applied canonical tag on the blog post pointing to the hire page
Expect Gold Coast rankings to improve as authority consolidates into one URL
